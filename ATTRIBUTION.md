## Current Arabic build source

The present `build_surahs.py` pipeline consumes `ara-quranuthmanihaf.json` (Quran Uthmani Hafs, Version 13). The edition metadata identifies its source as the King Fahd Quran Complex. Tanzil is used in Gold Master verification as an independent reference. Licensing/redistribution conclusions remain deferred to the separate legal review.

## Translations

Aggregated via `fawazahmed0/quran-api`, sourced from the Tanzil Project.
**An aggregator's licence does not launder the underlying terms.** Each
translation stands on its own.

### Cleared — public domain

- Marmaduke Pickthall, *The Meaning of the Glorious Koran* (1930)
- Abdullah Yusuf Ali, *The Holy Qur'an* (1934, original edition only — not the
  1946 Al-Rawaf printing, not the King Fahd revision)
- George Sale (1734), J. M. Rodwell (1861), E. H. Palmer (1880)

### Not cleared — confirm before shipping

- **The Monotheist Group** (2007) — methodologically the closest fit for this
  project. Terms unconfirmed.
- **Talal Itani** (2012) — widely described as freely distributable. Get it in
  writing.
- **Shabbir Ahmed** (2003) — terms unconfirmed.
- **M. H. Shakir** — copyright status contested; alleged to derive from earlier
  translations.

### Excluded deliberately

- **Saheeh International** — commonly described as free for non-commercial
  Islamic educational use. Donations, ads, or any paid tier would fall outside
  that.
- **Hilali-Khan** — licence unclear, and parenthetical content draws heavily on
  tafsir and hadith.
- **Abdel Haleem, Mustafa Khattab (Clear Quran), The Study Quran** — fully
  copyrighted.

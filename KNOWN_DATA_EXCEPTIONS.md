# Known Intentional Data Exceptions

## `l-w-t`
Retained as classical dictionary/reference material. It has zero canonical Quran occurrences. `لات` at 38:3 is handled as function word `l-a-t`.

## Quranic Unicode normalization
Quranic Arabic is not mechanically normalized to NFC. Combining-mark order and Quran-specific orthography are preserved unless a specific corruption is demonstrated.

## Compound segmentation
Some compound tokens legitimately map to more than one morphological component. Do not force all helper links to a single target.

## Verse-record convention
The current corpus has 6,236 explicit verse records. Internal IDs and references are certified against that repository convention.

## Classical dictionary breadth
Botanical, anatomical, medical, idiomatic, and other remote classical senses are not automatically errors. The product must distinguish classical lexical breadth from the contextual Quranic sense.

## Copyright/licensing
Not part of RC1 technical/linguistic certification and still requires separate review.
